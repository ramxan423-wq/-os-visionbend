# VisionBend Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

VisionBend is an electrical conduit bending calculator and planning application.

## Information we collect
VisionBend is designed to perform its core calculations on the device. It does not require an account and we do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, or calculation/project data through the app. Projects and settings may be stored locally on your device.

## Device permissions
If a device capability is requested by a supported feature, iOS controls access and you may decline or revoke permission in Settings. No permission is required simply to use the standard bending calculators.

## Payments
VisionBend is sold as a one-time paid download through Apple's App Store. Apple processes purchases. We do not receive your payment card details.

## Analytics and third parties
We do not intentionally use third-party advertising trackers. Apple may process App Store purchase and diagnostic information according to its own policies. Review the current App Store privacy label for app-specific disclosures.

## Data retention and security
Locally saved projects and preferences remain on your device until removed through the app or device management. We cannot access your local data remotely. You can delete the application and its local data using iOS controls.

## Children
VisionBend is a professional utility not specifically directed to children.

## Changes
We may update this policy as app features change. Updated text will be posted here.

## Contact
For privacy questions or support, please use the support link published on VisionBend's App Store product page.
