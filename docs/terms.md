## Field verification and safety
Before bending, cutting or installing conduit, verify measurements, units, conduit specifications, machine settings, bend allowances and take-up against the specific bender manufacturer's official instructions. Make a test bend when appropriate. Follow all applicable electrical codes, workplace safety rules and professional requirements.
