# VisionBend Support

VisionBend helps electricians, conduit installers and apprentices plan bends, estimate measurements and document projects.

## Getting started
1. Choose a calculator such as Offset, 3-Point Saddle, Kick or 90° Stub.
2. Select units and enter required measurements.
3. Calculate, then check the result against the bender manufacturer's reference and verify with a test bend before field installation.
4. Use the planning and projects areas to organize work where available.

## Troubleshooting
- Check that inputs use the intended units and positive, realistic dimensions.
- Verify conduit type, outside diameter, bender model, take-up and allowances.
- Restart the app if a screen becomes unresponsive; update to the latest available version.
- Calculations are planning aids and are not a substitute for manufacturer instructions or safe field practices.

## Contact support
Use the developer contact option on VisionBend's App Store listing to reach the support team. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, the screen/feature and steps to reproduce your issue. Do not share sensitive personal information.
